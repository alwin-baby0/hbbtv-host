### Added

<ul>

<li>Introduction of user login functionality with additional features: Continue Watching to resume viewing where you left off and Add content to your favorites list</li>
<li>Player: Redesigned with a more modern and functional interface</li>
<li>Integrated ads inside of player</li>
<li>Updated player ui - play/pause toggle button fix on media buttons</li>
<li>Updated hiding logic of subtitles container</li>
<li>Updated category bar items text length</li>
<li>Updated subtitle button opacity</li>
<li>Updated app exit text length</li>
<li>Updated title container widths on movies and cmp</li>
<li>New exit dialogue screen</li>
</ul>
